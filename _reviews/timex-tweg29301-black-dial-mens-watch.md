## Key Features Explained

### 1. Classic Black Round Dial

The watch features a black round dial paired with a matching black stainless steel bracelet. This gives it a clean, understated appearance that can work with casual outfits, office wear and smart-casual clothing.

Its 44mm case creates a noticeable wrist presence, making it a suitable option for people who prefer larger watches.

### 2. Convenient Day-Date Display

The day-date feature lets you check the day and date along with the time.

It is a practical addition for everyday use, particularly if you prefer having basic calendar information directly on your wrist.

### 3. Quartz Movement for Accurate Timekeeping

The TIMEX TWEG29301 uses a battery-powered quartz movement.

Quartz watches are generally valued for their dependable timekeeping and low-maintenance operation. You do not need to wind the watch manually like a traditional mechanical watch.

### 4. Stainless Steel Case and Bracelet

Both the case and bracelet are made from stainless steel, giving the watch a coordinated appearance and a solid everyday feel.

The black finish adds to its modern styling. However, like other coated metal watches, the finish may show signs of wear or scratches with regular use.

### 5. 50-Metre Water Resistance

The watch is listed with 50-metre water resistance.

This offers protection for everyday water exposure and some recreational water activities, subject to the manufacturer's usage guidelines. It should not automatically be treated as suitable for diving or high-pressure water activities.

### 6. Mineral Glass Crystal

The dial is protected by mineral glass, which offers everyday protection against minor impacts and scratches.

However, mineral glass is generally less scratch-resistant than sapphire crystal, so reasonable care is recommended.

### 7. Foldover Clasp with Push Button

The stainless steel bracelet uses a foldover clasp with a push-button mechanism.

This design helps keep the watch securely fastened while making it convenient to open and close.
