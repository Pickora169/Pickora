The product listing mentions six ports, offering additional flexibility when charging compatible devices.

The built-in digital display shows the remaining battery percentage.

This makes it easier to decide when the power bank needs recharging.

Its listed weight is 200g, making it reasonably portable for everyday carrying.

The built-in cables also reduce the need to carry additional accessories separately.

### 7. Lithium Polymer Battery

The device uses a lithium polymer battery cell.

This battery technology is commonly used in portable power banks and other rechargeable electronics.

As with any power bank, use a compatible charger, avoid excessive heat and follow the manufacturer's safety instructions.
