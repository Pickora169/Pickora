## boAt Stone 510 Bluetooth Speaker: Overview

The boAt Stone 510 is a portable Bluetooth speaker designed for people who want enjoyable music without depending on their phone's built-in speaker. With 25W RMS output, an advertised battery life of up to 8 hours and an IPX5-rated body, it is designed for both indoor listening and outdoor activities.

Its Midnight Black finish and ergonomic carry handle give it a practical portable-speaker design. Features such as TWS pairing, Type-C charging and a built-in microphone add to its everyday convenience.

## Key Features Explained

### 1. 25W boAt Signature Sound

The speaker offers 25W RMS output and is designed to deliver punchy bass, clear vocals and dynamic sound. This output should be suitable for personal listening, rooms and small outdoor gatherings.

### 2. Up to 8 Hours of Battery Life

boAt claims up to 8 hours of playback on a single charge. This can be useful for day trips, casual gatherings and extended listening sessions. Actual battery performance may vary depending on volume, audio content and usage.

### 3. IPX5 Water Resistance

The IPX5 rating provides protection against water splashes and sweat. It makes the speaker more convenient for poolside use, workouts and light rain exposure. However, it should not be submerged in water.

### 4. TWS Mode for Two-Speaker Pairing

One of its useful features is True Wireless Stereo (TWS) mode. You can pair two compatible boAt Stone 510 speakers to create a combined 50W RMS setup with left and right stereo separation.

Keep in mind that you need a second compatible speaker to enjoy this feature.

### 5. Built-in Microphone and Voice Assistant

The built-in microphone allows hands-free calling when connected to a compatible phone. Voice assistant support also adds convenience when you want to access supported phone assistant functions.

### 6. Type-C Charging and Carry Handle

Type-C charging makes everyday charging more convenient, while the ergonomic carry handle helps you move the speaker around more easily.
