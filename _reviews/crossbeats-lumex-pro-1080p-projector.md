## Crossbeats Lumex Pro Projector: Overview

The Crossbeats Lumex Pro is a portable LED projector designed for people who want a big-screen entertainment experience at home without investing in an expensive home cinema setup.

It features native Full HD 1920 x 1080 resolution, Android 14 built-in apps, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity, auto focus and auto keystone correction. The projector also advertises a maximum screen size of up to 300 inches.

Its gold finish and portable form factor make it suitable for tabletop placement, while the built-in speaker offers a convenient audio option for casual viewing.

One important point to understand is that the projector supports 4K content but has a native resolution of 1080p. It does not display native 4K resolution.

## Key Features Explained

### 1. Native Full HD 1080p Resolution

The Lumex Pro offers a native resolution of 1920 x 1080 pixels, which is Full HD.

This resolution is suitable for watching movies, web series, sports and online videos on a large screen. It can provide a more detailed picture than basic 720p projectors.

The listing also mentions 4K support. However, this refers to compatibility with 4K content rather than native 4K projection. The actual projected resolution remains Full HD.

### 2. Advertised 14,000 Lumens Brightness

Crossbeats advertises 14,000 lumens of brightness, which sounds impressive on paper.

However, projector brightness specifications can be presented using different measurement standards. The listing does not clearly establish whether this figure represents ANSI lumens or another measurement.

For this reason, buyers should not assume that the projector will deliver excellent visibility in direct daylight. For better picture quality, a darker room or controlled lighting environment is generally recommended.

### 3. Android 14 with Built-in Apps

One of the major attractions is the advertised Android 14 operating system with built-in apps.

This can make streaming and entertainment more convenient without always connecting an external streaming device.

However, app availability, compatibility, certification and streaming resolution should be checked before purchasing, especially if you regularly use services such as Netflix or Prime Video.

### 4. Auto Focus and Auto Keystone Correction

The projector includes auto focus and auto keystone features.

Auto focus helps adjust image sharpness, while auto keystone correction helps correct the projected image shape when the projector is not perfectly aligned with the screen.

These features can reduce manual adjustments and make setup easier.

### 5. Large Display up to 300 Inches

The manufacturer advertises a maximum projection size of up to 300 inches.

This makes the projector appealing for movie nights and home entertainment. However, the actual image size and quality depend on projection distance, brightness and room conditions.

A smaller screen may provide a more comfortable and brighter viewing experience than using the maximum advertised size.

### 6. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Connectivity

The Lumex Pro supports Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, along with HDMI, USB and 3.5mm audio connectivity.

This allows users to connect compatible smartphones, laptops, gaming consoles and other devices.

Bluetooth connectivity can also be useful when connecting compatible external audio equipment.

### 7. Built-in Speaker

The built-in speaker provides a convenient audio solution without requiring an additional speaker for basic viewing.

For a more immersive movie experience, connecting a dedicated external speaker or soundbar may offer better sound quality.

### 8. Portable Design and Long Light Source Life

With a listed weight of approximately 1.28 kg, the Lumex Pro is relatively easy to move between rooms.

Its LED light source is advertised to last up to 50,000 hours. Actual lifespan depends on operating conditions and usage.
