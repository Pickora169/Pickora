## Key Features Explained

### 1. 60W Super Fast Charging 2.0

The adapter delivers up to 60W of power for compatible devices. Samsung states that it can charge the Galaxy S26 Ultra to 75% in approximately 30 minutes under supported charging conditions.

Actual charging speed depends on the device, battery level, temperature and compatible charging cable.

### 2. GaN Technology for Efficient Charging

GaN stands for Gallium Nitride, a semiconductor technology used in modern power adapters.

It helps deliver efficient power in a smaller form factor compared with many traditional charger designs. This makes the adapter convenient to carry in a pocket or travel bag.

### 3. USB Power Delivery 3.1 with PPS

The charger supports USB Power Delivery 3.1 and Programmable Power Supply (PPS).

PPS allows compatible devices to adjust charging voltage and current dynamically. This helps optimise charging performance for supported smartphones and other devices.

It is important to check your device's charging specifications before purchasing, as not every device supports the same fast-charging standards.

### 4. Compatibility with Multiple Devices

The Samsung 60W adapter can charge compatible smartphones, tablets and laptops through its USB-C port.

This makes it a useful option for people who want a single charger for several types of devices.

However, it has only one USB-C port, so you can charge only one device at a time.

### 5. Low Standby Power Consumption

Samsung lists standby power consumption at just 0.005W.

This helps minimise electricity consumption when the adapter remains plugged in without actively charging a device.

### 6. Recycled Materials and Safety Features

According to the product description, the adapter incorporates 20% recycled material and is UL certified.

It also lists short-circuit protection, adding another layer of protection during charging.
