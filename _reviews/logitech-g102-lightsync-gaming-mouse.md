## Key Features Explained

### 1. Gaming-Grade 8000 DPI Sensor

The Logitech G102 features an optical gaming sensor with sensitivity settings of up to 8000 DPI.

You can customize sensitivity through Logitech G HUB and switch between up to five DPI settings.

This makes it useful for different gaming styles, whether you prefer slower, controlled movements or faster cursor movements during gameplay.

### 2. LIGHTSYNC RGB Lighting

One of the most noticeable features is its customizable RGB lighting.

The mouse supports approximately 16.8 million colours, allowing you to personalize its appearance through Logitech G HUB.

You can choose preset lighting effects or create your own colour combinations. It also supports game-driven lighting, audio visualization and screen mapping options.

### 3. Six Programmable Buttons

The G102 comes with a simple six-button layout that provides additional control during gaming.

Each button can be customized using Logitech G HUB to assign different commands, shortcuts or functions.

This can be useful in games that require quick access to multiple commands, as well as everyday tasks where shortcuts can improve productivity.

### 4. Mechanical Button Tensioning

Logitech uses mechanical button tensioning for the primary mouse buttons.

This design helps provide crisp clicks and consistent feedback, making the mouse feel responsive during repeated clicking.

It is particularly useful for games where accurate and quick mouse inputs matter.

### 5. Lightweight and Comfortable Design

At approximately 85g, the G102 is relatively lightweight for a wired gaming mouse.

Its familiar gaming shape is designed to provide comfortable control during extended use.

However, comfort can depend on your hand size and preferred grip style.

### 6. Logitech G HUB Software

Logitech G HUB is the software used to customize the mouse.

Through the software, you can:
- Adjust DPI sensitivity settings
- Switch between up to five DPI levels
- Customize the six programmable buttons
- Personalize RGB lighting effects
- Configure lighting and other supported settings

This gives users more control over the mouse compared with a basic plug-and-play model.
