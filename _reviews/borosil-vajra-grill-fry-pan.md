## Who Should Skip It?

Consider other options if you:

- Regularly prepare large quantities of food.
- Prefer stainless-steel or cast-iron cookware without a non-stick coating.

## Care and Maintenance Tips

To help maintain the non-stick surface:

- Cook on low to medium heat.
- Avoid heating the empty pan for extended periods.
- Allow the pan to cool before washing.
- Clean with a soft sponge and soapy water.
- Avoid abrasive cleaners and wire scrubbers.
- Follow the manufacturer's care instructions.
