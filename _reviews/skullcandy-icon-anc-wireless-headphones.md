## Key Features Explained

### 1. Active Noise Cancelling and Stay-Aware Mode

The Icon ANC comes with Active Noise Cancelling to help reduce unwanted background sounds while listening to music, watching videos or working.

It also offers an adjustable Stay-Aware mode, which lets you hear more of your surroundings when needed. This can be useful when walking outdoors, commuting or listening for announcements.

### 2. Up to 60 Hours of Battery Life

Battery life is one of the major highlights of these headphones. Skullcandy advertises up to 60 hours of playback, making them suitable for extended listening sessions without frequent charging.

However, the supplied product specifications also mention a 50-hour average battery life. Actual playback time may vary depending on volume, ANC usage and other settings.

### 3. Rapid Charging

The rapid-charge feature is particularly useful when you are short on time. According to the product description, a 10-minute charge can provide up to four additional hours of music.

This is convenient for travel, daily commuting and situations where you forget to charge your headphones in advance.

### 4. Lightweight and Foldable Design

The foldable construction makes the Icon ANC easier to store in a bag when travelling or commuting.

Its cushioned on-ear design is intended for everyday comfort. However, comfort is subjective, and some people may prefer over-ear headphones for longer listening sessions.

### 5. IPX4 Water and Sweat Resistance

The IPX4 rating provides protection against water splashes from different directions. This makes the headphones more suitable for light workouts, sweat and occasional rain exposure.

They are not waterproof and should not be submerged in water.

### 6. Custom EQ with Skullcandy App

The Skullcandy app allows users to personalise their listening experience through preset or custom equaliser settings.

This is useful if you prefer more bass, clearer vocals or a different sound profile depending on the type of content you enjoy.

### 7. Built-in Microphone and Voice Assistant

The integrated microphone supports hands-free calls, while voice assistant access adds convenience when using a compatible smartphone.
