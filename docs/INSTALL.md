PICKORA - Phase 1 & 2 install guide

1. In WordPress: Plugins > Add New > Upload > pickora-core.zip > Activate.
2. Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload > pickora.zip > Activate.
3. Settings > Permalinks > choose "Post name" > Save.
4. Settings > PICKORA: enter your Amazon Associates tracking ID, social links, contact email.
5. Settings > Reading: "Your homepage displays" = A static page (create an empty page, e.g. "Home").
   The theme's front-page.php takes over automatically.
6. Appearance > Menus: create a menu and assign to "Primary menu" (or use the built-in default).

Publishing a review: Reviews > Add New Review.
 - Title, main editor (full review), Excerpt (short intro), Featured image (product photo)
 - Pick a Review Category on the right
 - Fill "Review details" below the editor. Specs use "Label | Value" per line; FAQs use "Question | Answer".
 - Leave any field blank to hide that section on the article.
 - Publish. It appears on the homepage, category page, search and related reviews automatically.

Featured review: Settings > PICKORA > Homepage featured review.
Editor's Picks: tick the box in "Review details". The section hides until you tick at least one.
Pages to create (slugs matter for footer links): about-us, our-review-methodology, editorial-policy,
affiliate-disclosure, contact-us, privacy-policy, terms-and-conditions.

PHASE 3 ADDITIONS
Guides menu: Guides > Add New Guide. Pick a Guide Type (Buying Guides, Comparisons, Best Picks, Deals)
and optionally a Budget (Under Rs 500 etc.). Add more budgets under Guides > Budgets.
Homepage budget, comparison and guide sections appear automatically once you have published content for them.
Settings > PICKORA > "Create starter pages" creates About, Methodology, Editorial Policy, Affiliate Disclosure,
Contact (with working form) and draft Privacy/Terms templates. See DEPLOY.txt for launch and backup notes.

PHASE 4 ADDITIONS
- "Last updated date" field in Review details. Blank = automatic date of your last save. Used on the article and in schema.
- "Alternatives" and "Related articles" are now pick-lists of your published reviews (Ctrl/Cmd-click for several).
  Related articles you pick show first; remaining slots fill automatically from the same category.
- Logo files: pickora-profile-1080.png (Instagram/Facebook/YouTube profile image), pickora-icon-512.png (favicon / Site Icon),
  pickora-icon.svg. Upload the 512 PNG at Settings > General > Site Icon.
