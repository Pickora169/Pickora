## Research-based reviews
These reviews are based on manufacturer specifications, published information and available user and expert feedback. We have not used the product ourselves, and each article says so.

## Hands-on tested reviews
These are only used when we have actually used the product. Each article carries a "Hands-on tested" label and describes what we did.

## Ratings
We do not publish star ratings. If we introduce them, we will document exactly how they are calculated here first.
